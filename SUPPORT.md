# Getting help with TaskPop

## Look here first

- [Troubleshooting](docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md) covers the double-tap, shortcuts, notifications, installing, updating and getting lost tasks back.
- [Permissions](docs/PERMISSIONS.md) explains every permission TaskPop asks for.
- [Privacy](docs/PRIVACY.md) says what TaskPop stores and when it goes online.
- The [changelog](CHANGELOG.md) says what changed in each version. Updating to the [latest release](https://github.com/asmrayat/Task-pop/releases/latest) fixes many problems.

## Ask or report

- **Something isn't working:** [open a bug report](https://github.com/asmrayat/Task-pop/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Include your TaskPop version (**Settings → Updates**), your macOS or Windows version, and the steps that make it happen.
- **An idea or a missing feature:** [suggest it](https://github.com/asmrayat/Task-pop/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **A security or privacy problem:** please don't open a public issue. Report it privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Before opening a new issue, [search the existing ones](https://github.com/asmrayat/Task-pop/issues?q=is%3Aissue): someone may already have asked, and a 👍 on that issue helps more than a duplicate.

Answers can take a few days. Thanks for your patience.
