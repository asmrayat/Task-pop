Welcome to TaskPop

A small task list that slides in from the side of your screen.

  • Double-tap Control to open it from any app. Pick Option, Command or Shift instead in Settings.
  • Or press Control + Option + T, or say “Hey Siri, open TaskPop”.
  • It pops up by itself when you open your MacBook’s lid.
  • Send any task to Google Calendar: click ⋯ on the task, pick a date and time, and it opens in your browser ready to save.
  • When a new version is out, TaskPop asks you with a small pop-up: update now, or later.
  • Your tasks stay on this Mac. TaskPop never uses your microphone.

Stay connected to the internet while it installs. A first install downloads the rest of TaskPop (about 130 MB), which takes a minute or two. Updating from an earlier version keeps your tasks and settings and usually needs no download.
